# Product Vision Statement

## One Click OffGrid

- FOR privacy-conscious individuals who have built up years of online accounts, subscriptions, and personal data scattered across the web
- WHO have lost track of where their personal information lives and find it overwhelming and time-consuming to locate and delete it manually, site by site
- THE One Click OffGrid IS A AI-powered digital footprint analysis and removal tool for laptops and phones
- THAT scans your connected email accounts, browsing history, and images to map your entire digital footprint, then automatically unsubscribes you and requests removal of your data from websites, all with a single click
- UNLIKE manual account deletion or data-broker removal services such as DeleteMe or Incogni, which only cover a fixed list of broker sites
- OUR PRODUCT discovers the accounts and data you actually have by analyzing your own inbox and history, uses image matching to find similar content about you online, and handles both discovery and removal in one automated process

## Full Statement

For privacy-conscious individuals who have lost track of where their personal information lives online, One Click OffGrid is an AI-powered digital footprint analysis and removal tool that maps your entire online presence using your emails, browsing history, and images, then removes it with a single click. Unlike data-broker removal services that only cover a fixed list of sites, our product finds the accounts and data that are specific to you and handles both discovery and removal automatically.
